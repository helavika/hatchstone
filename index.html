      Trouble connecting?
      

        
Links connect through the free PeerJS service, then straight between your two devices. Your friend can see your network address; there's no camera or microphone. Some networks block direct connections. Invites expire after 10 minutes.

        
If links don't work, swap codes by hand instead. This skips PeerJS.
